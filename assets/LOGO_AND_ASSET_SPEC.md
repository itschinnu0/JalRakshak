Direction: premium minimalist JalRakshak mark combining a clean water droplet, protective shield and subtle technology motif. Aquatic blue/teal, restrained gradient, strong negative space, geometric balance. Must work at app-icon size and in light/dark themes.

Avoid generic water-company imagery, literal microchips, Arduino imagery, gears, complex circuitry and cartoon styling.

Wordmark: JalRakshak. Optional subtitle: Smart Water Purification & Monitoring.

Prefer vector/density-independent assets. Decorative assets must never be required for core functionality.
